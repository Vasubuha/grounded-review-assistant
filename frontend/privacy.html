Privacy Policy

RAG Product Assistant does not collect, store, sell, or share personal user information.

Information Usage

The extension temporarily accesses publicly visible product page content, including product specifications and reviews from supported e-commerce websites, only to provide AI-powered product analysis and question answering features.

Data Storage

The extension may store local settings and cached product data inside the user's browser using Chrome storage APIs. No personal information is transmitted or sold to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The extension communicates with backend AI services and vector databases required for product analysis functionality.

Permissions

The extension uses browser permissions such as activeTab, scripting, tabs, storage, sidePanel, and host permissions only for extension functionality.
